# Forex Lot Management System — User & Operator Guide

Welcome to **FOREX OS**, an enterprise currency lot management system engineered for foreign exchange departments, treasury desks, and commercial currency operations.

---

## 1. Quick Navigation
- **Command Palette**: Press `⌘ K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows) from any screen to instantly open navigation shortcuts, search active lots, create transactions, or switch operational views.
- **Top Search**: Instant live grouping search across lots, currencies, sales, and purchases.
- **Notification Center**: Bell icon in header displays real-time compliance alerts (e.g. currencies operating below minimum reserve threshold).

---

## 2. Executive Dashboard (`/`)
Provides real-time answers to the core operator questions:
1. **WHAT DO I HAVE?**: Vault positions breakdown showing remaining units and stock valuations.
2. **WHAT DID I BUY? & WHAT DID I SELL?**: Live feed of completed acquisitions and client sales.
3. **HOW MUCH PROFIT DID I MAKE?**: Realized Profit KPI card with percentage period comparison.
4. **WHAT NEEDS MY ATTENTION?**: Low-stock alerts and reconciliation mismatch indicators.

---

## 3. Recording a Currency Purchase (`/purchases`)
To add currency stock and generate a new traceable lot:
1. Click **+ New Purchase** on the top right.
2. **Step 01 - Currency**: Select the foreign currency (e.g. USD, EUR, AED).
3. **Step 02 - Quantity**: Enter the number of units to acquire (or click quick presets `+100`, `+500`, etc.).
4. **Step 03 - Price**: Specify the purchase unit rate in base currency (e.g. ₹94.50). Counterparty and deal ID can optionally be noted.
5. **Step 04 - Review**: Verify the total spend calculation, acquisition rate, and sequential lot assignment.
6. Click **Confirm Purchase**. A unique lot (e.g. `LOT-1007`) is assigned immediately with 100% stock available in the vault.

---

## 4. Executing a Currency Sale (`/sales`)
1. Click **+ New Sale**.
2. **Step 01 - Currency**: Choose the currency being sold.
3. **Step 02 - Quantity**: Enter units requested by the client.
4. **Step 03 - Lot Allocation**:
   - **FIFO (Recommended)**: Automatically draws from oldest active lots first, previewing the exact units deducted from each lot.
   - **MANUAL**: Choose specific individual lot cards by clicking to toggle selection and allocating exact units per lot.
5. **Step 04 - Sell Price & Live Profit**:
   - Enter your client selling price per unit.
   - The **Live Profit Calculator** displays unit spread and exact expected profit for each contributing lot.
6. **Step 05 - Review**: Review client totals, expected realized profit, and allocated lot deductions.
7. Click **Confirm Sale**.

---

## 5. Inspecting Individual Lot Lifecycles (`/lots/[id]`)
Clicking any lot number (e.g. `LOT-1001`) opens the Lot Detail screen:
- **UNITS REMAINING**: Large typography showing current vault balance.
- **Cost Metrics**: Initial unit cost, original quantity, units sold, and total realized profit generated by this lot.
- **Movement Timeline**: Chronological visual ledger documenting every inbound purchase, sales allocation deduction, reversal, or adjustment with balance progression after each event.

---

## 6. Reversing a Confirmed Sale
If a trade fails wire settlement or is cancelled:
1. In `/sales`, click on the transaction row to slide open the **Sale Detail Drawer**.
2. Click **Reverse Sale & Restore Lots**.
3. Enter a mandatory audited justification.
4. The system restores currency back to original lots, reverses profit accounting, and logs an immutable reversal audit record.

---

## 7. Inventory Reconciliation (`/reconciliation`)
1. Compare **Expected Stock** (mathematical result of purchases minus sales plus adjustments) against **Actual Stock** (sum of remaining units across physical lots).
2. If variance is 0, status displays **Balanced**.
3. If variance exists, click **Create Adjustment** to record a compliant stock adjustment to the physical lot.
